Acreditation of English Education Study Program

30 January 2026 Firda Zulkarnia

The English Education Study Program at Universitas PGRI Wiranegara conducted its accreditation process on January 22–23, 2026. This activity became an important step in improving the quality of education and academic standards within the study program. The field assessment was carried out by two experienced LAMDIK assessors, namely Dr. Jumariati, M.Pd. and Santthy Hwanti, S.Pd., M.Hum., PhD.

The accreditation process was led by the Head of the English Education Study Program, Ana Ahsana El-Sulukiyyah, S.Pd., M.Pd., together with all lecturers of the English Education Study Program, lecturers from other study programs, students, and alumni who actively participated in supporting the entire assessment process. The event was also attended by the Rector, Dr. Mochammad Taufiq, M.Pd., along with lecturers and other university officials from Universitas PGRI Wiranegara.

During the assessment, the assessors reviewed various important aspects, including curriculum, teaching and learning processes, supporting facilities, lecturers’ performance, as well as students’ and alumni achievements. All academic members worked together and showed strong commitment to achieving the best results for the study program.

The accreditation result of the English Education Study Program was officially announced with the achievement of the “Very Good” accreditation status. This accomplishment proves the hard work, dedication, and commitment of all parties in improving the quality of education within the English Education Study Program at Universitas PGRI Wiranegara. It is hoped that this achievement will motivate the program to continue developing and producing excellent, professional, and competitive graduates in the field of education and beyond.
