Welcome to Our Website

The English Education Study Program at Universitas PGRI Wiranegara equips students with strong language skills, modern teaching abilities, and digital literacy. Supported by dedicated lecturers and practical learning experiences, the program prepares graduates to inspire and excel in both national and global education.

About Us

We are ESA (English Students Association) of Universitas PGRI Wiranegara — a dynamic student organization dedicated to fostering academic excellence and cross-cultural communication through English. Our mission is to empower students with global competencies and leadership skills.

Since our establishment, we’ve hosted national seminars, English competitions, and community engagement programs to inspire students to explore the limitless potential of language and education.
