Third Winner at DEMAWA 2025

28 December 2025 Fadilah Istiqomah

The ESA team, consisting of three members, participated in a competition organized by the PPKN Study Program Student Association (HMPS PPKN). This participation is a form of ESA team's contribution and active participation in academic and competitive activities on campus.

Before entering the preliminary round, the ESA team conducted several training sessions as part of their initial preparation. These training sessions focused on the learning process, understanding the material, and improving cooperation among team members. This gradual preparation was an important asset in facing the competition.

The preliminary round was held online. Thanks to their efforts, discipline, and thorough preparation, the ESA team was able to perform well in the preliminary round and successfully advanced to the next round. This success became additional motivation for the team to continue striving for the best results.

Next, the ESA team continued the competition to the final round, which was held offline yesterday on December 27, 2025. At this stage, the competition became more intense and demanded maximum mental preparedness and ability from each team member.

Through hard work, teamwork, and the strategies prepared beforehand, the ESA team secured third place in the competition. This achievement is a source of pride and demonstrates that consistent training and well-planned preparation can lead to outstanding results.
